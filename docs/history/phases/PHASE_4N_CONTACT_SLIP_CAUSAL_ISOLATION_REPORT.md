## 1. Executive Summary

`slip1=0.035` on both production drive wheels is a causal contributor to the
observed physical-vs-odometry mismatch in this Gazebo model.

Fresh baseline A reproduced the Phase 4M signature:

- turn under-rotation: approximately `17.74°` in both directions;
- forward/reverse post-stop displacement: approximately `0.070–0.073 m` in
  the dominant D1/D2 segments;
- wheel/controller outputs remained kinematically correct.

Disposable candidate B changed only both drive-wheel `slip1` values from
`0.035` to `0.0`. Across three valid repeats per direction, turn error fell
to below `0.60°`; straight-motion physical-vs-odometry error fell below
`0.67%`; and the post-stop displacement fell to approximately `0.038 m`
forward and `0.040 m` reverse.

This is causal evidence, not a production fix. The production SDF stayed
frozen and the motion gate remains `BLOCKED`.
