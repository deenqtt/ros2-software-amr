## 1. Executive Summary

Status: BLOCKED

Final decision: **PHASE 4M: FIRST DIVERGENCE IDENTIFIED — ROOT CAUSE PARTIAL — PRODUCTION STILL BLOCKED**.

The continuous controlled trace shows that mirrored CCW/CW commands, wheel velocities, wheel positions, Gazebo ground-truth yaw, and primary contact forces are substantially mirrored. The earlier interpretation of a uniquely broken CCW direction was not reproduced under this controlled continuous method. Instead, both physical turn directions under-rotate relative to `/odom` by approximately 18 degrees after the same wheel motion.

The first defensible divergence is bounded between the wheel-joint layer and physical chassis/contact response: wheel targets and wheel joint trajectories are correct, while independent Gazebo physical yaw falls behind the DiffDrive odometry model. Contact telemetry shows all four primary supports active and no measurable CCW/CW front-caster asymmetry. This identifies the physical contact/constraint/slip response as the relevant subsystem, but does not prove one exact constitutive parameter as the root cause.

For stop dynamics, the wheel reaches the near-zero threshold in approximately `0.38–0.40 s` after zero command, but the body continues moving for approximately `1.60–1.72 s` and travels approximately `0.071–0.075 m` after wheel stop. Wheel angular displacement after T3 is negligible while contact remains active and caster normal force redistributes during braking. The best-supported explanation is body/contact settling and residual physical body motion, not continued wheel rotation alone.

Production remains unchanged. Phase 5 was not started.

## 33. Stop Root-Cause Assessment

Status: BLOCKED  
Evidence: INFERRED from VERIFIED AT RUNTIME evidence

Best-supported stop cause: physical body/contact settling or residual body dynamics after wheel velocity becomes negligible, with caster normal-force redistribution during braking. It is not adequately described as ordinary wheel coasting because wheel angular displacement after T3 is negligible.

Contact stop traces showed all four primary contacts active at T2, T3, T4, and T5. Forward caster force shifted to approximately `53.9 N` while rear shifted to `43.8 N` at T3; reverse shifted oppositely to approximately `46.0 N` front and `51.6 N` rear. This supports contact-load redistribution, but does not prove whether the remaining body displacement is solver settling, compliance, slip, or another contact mechanism.
